2. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Information of Readers

We do not require general readers or students to register an account or provide personal information (such as name, phone number, address, or contacts) to read Quran, Hadith, Duas, or Islamic educational articles.

B. On-Device Storage

Your bookmarks, reading progress, quiz scores, font size preferences, and theme settings are saved locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore / Room. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share a post.

C. Administrator Authentication

For designated content administrators who manage articles via the admin portal, we utilize Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication. Only verified administrative emails (e.g., authorized editorial team) receive database publishing permissions.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

To maintain the free availability of Noor Daily and support continuous content research, our application uses trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google:

  • Google AdMob: We serve ethical, policy-compliant banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP addresses, and crash telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Used for core platform functionality, app updates, and identity services.
  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Used for secure cloud database hosting (Cloud Firestore) to deliver verified Quranic and Hadith texts to the app.
You can review Google’s privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Information Sharing.

4. Children's Privacy (শিশুদের সুরক্ষা)

Noor Daily features a dedicated "Kids Islamic Education" section with Kalimas, Wudu steps, and moral stories. We do not collect any personal data from children under the age of 13. All content is strictly educational, wholesome, and family-friendly.

5. Data Security & Storage

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and secure Firebase rules to ensure that data communication between the app and our verified content database is safe against unauthorized interception or tampering.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to request information regarding any data associated with your interaction with Noor Daily. Since readers do not create cloud accounts, you can delete all locally cached data at any time simply by clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

If you have questions or wish to request data deletion, please contact our support team at: habitsk50@gmail.com.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and providing notice within the app settings.
